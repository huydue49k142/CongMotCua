# Brainstorming

## Overview

A structured facilitation skill for conducting effective brainstorming sessions. Guides the process of generating, organizing, and evaluating ideas through systematic ideation techniques and creative problem-solving methods.

## When to Use

- Generating creative solutions to complex problems
- Conducting product discovery and feature ideation
- Exploring multiple approaches to technical challenges
- Facilitating team brainstorming sessions
- Developing innovative strategies and roadmaps

## Workflow

### Step 1: Define the Challenge
Clearly articulate the problem or opportunity, setting constraints and success criteria.

### Step 2: Ideation Generation
Apply structured ideation techniques (mind mapping, SCAMPER, reverse thinking) to generate diverse ideas.

### Step 3: Organization & Clustering
Group related ideas, identify themes, and create affinity maps for evaluation.

### Step 4: Prioritization
Evaluate ideas against success criteria and prioritize for further exploration or implementation.
