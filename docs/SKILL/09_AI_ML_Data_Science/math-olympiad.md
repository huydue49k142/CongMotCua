# Math Olympiad

## Overview

A skill for solving advanced mathematical problems at olympiad and competition level. Covers number theory, algebra, geometry, combinatorics, and complex analytical reasoning with step-by-step solution strategies.

## When to Use

- Solving advanced mathematical competition problems
- Developing rigorous mathematical proofs
- Analyzing complex mathematical structures and patterns
- Teaching advanced mathematical concepts
- Preparing for mathematical olympiad competitions

## Workflow

### Step 1: Problem Analysis
Analyze the problem statement, identify the mathematical domain, and determine the type of solution required.

### Step 2: Strategy Formulation
Develop a solution strategy based on known theorems, techniques, and problem-solving heuristics.

### Step 3: Solution Execution
Execute the solution with rigorous step-by-step reasoning and proper mathematical notation.

### Step 4: Verification
Verify the solution through alternative methods, edge cases, and logical consistency checks.
