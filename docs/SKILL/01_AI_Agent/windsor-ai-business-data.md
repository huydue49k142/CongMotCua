# Windsor AI Business Data

## Overview

A skill for leveraging Windsor AI to extract, analyze, and derive business intelligence from company data. Covers business data enrichment, competitive analysis, market research, and automated data pipeline creation.

## When to Use

- Enriching business data with company intelligence
- Performing competitive analysis and market research
- Extracting structured business data from unstructured sources
- Building automated business data pipelines
- Generating business insights and reports

## Workflow

### Step 1: Data Source Configuration
Connect Windsor AI to relevant business data sources and define extraction parameters.

### Step 2: Data Extraction
Extract structured business data including company profiles, financials, and market positioning.

### Step 3: Analysis & Enrichment
Analyze extracted data for patterns, trends, and actionable business intelligence.

### Step 4: Reporting & Integration
Generate reports and integrate enriched data into business systems and workflows.
