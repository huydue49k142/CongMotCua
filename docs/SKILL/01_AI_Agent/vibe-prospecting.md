# Vibe Prospecting

## Overview

A skill for automating sales prospecting and lead research using AI agents. Covers prospect identification, company research, personalized outreach preparation, and pipeline management for sales development workflows.

## When to Use

- Automating prospect research and lead qualification
- Generating personalized sales outreach content
- Building targeted prospect lists from multiple data sources
- Enriching CRM data with prospect insights
- Preparing for sales meetings with prospect background research

## Workflow

### Step 1: Define Ideal Prospect Profile
Define target criteria including industry, company size, role, and buying signals.

### Step 2: Prospect Discovery
Search across data sources to identify prospects matching the ideal profile.

### Step 3: Research & Enrichment
Research each prospect for relevant context, pain points, and engagement opportunities.

### Step 4: Outreach Preparation
Generate personalized outreach templates and talking points based on prospect research.
