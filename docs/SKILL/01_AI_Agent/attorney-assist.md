# Attorney Assist

## Overview

An AI-powered assistant designed to help legal professionals with document analysis, legal research, case preparation, and contract review. Streamlines legal workflows by automating routine legal tasks while maintaining high accuracy and compliance standards.

## When to Use

- Need to analyze and summarize legal documents and contracts
- Performing legal research across case law and statutes
- Preparing case materials and legal arguments
- Reviewing contracts for potential issues and risks
- Drafting legal correspondence and filings

## Workflow

### Step 1: Document Ingestion
Upload or reference legal documents for analysis including contracts, pleadings, discovery materials, and correspondence.

### Step 2: Analysis & Extraction
The assistant analyzes documents to extract key clauses, obligations, dates, parties, and potential risk areas.

### Step 3: Research & Validation
Cross-reference findings with relevant case law, statutes, and regulations to validate interpretations.

### Step 4: Output Generation
Generate summaries, red flags reports, comparison matrices, or draft responses as needed.
